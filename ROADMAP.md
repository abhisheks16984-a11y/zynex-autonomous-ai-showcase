# ZYNEX Roadmap

This roadmap communicates direction, not delivery dates. Any item may change as product validation, security review, and technical feasibility evolve.

## V1 — Foundation

The current web foundation focuses on AI chat, account-aware experiences, document and website analysis, technical assistance, subscriptions, and secure product operations.

## V2 — Intelligence Expansion

Potential work includes richer research workflows, improved personalization, expanded productivity assistance, and additional cross-device experiences.

## V3 — Autonomous Capabilities

Planned or research-stage themes include goal decomposition, tool-aware workflows, self-audit concepts, mission-oriented execution, and intelligent decision support. These concepts are not production guarantees.

## Future — Advanced AI Ecosystem

Longer-term research may explore advanced memory, multi-agent collaboration, AI shopping intelligence, advanced automation, and multi-node or consensus research. Status is intentionally exploratory.
